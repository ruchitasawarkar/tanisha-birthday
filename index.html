Happy 18th birthday, little one! ❤️

I still remember the little girl you were, and now look at you — officially 18! Time really needs to slow down. Although, honestly, I still think you're the same little kid who can annoy me in record time. 😂

We may fight over the smallest things, tease each other for no reason and sometimes disagree on absolutely everything. But underneath all that teasing, you will always have a special place in my heart.

I want you to dream big, believe in yourself and never stop trying. Life won't always be easy, but never think that you have to figure everything out in one day. Take your time, learn, grow and enjoy every little moment.

I hope you always find reasons to smile, people who genuinely care about you and opportunities that help you become the person you dream of being.

And one important thing: no matter how grown-up you become, your Didi will still have the right to tease you, give you advice and remind you to behave! 😂

Thank you for being my sister, my partner in memories and sometimes my favourite headache.

Love you more than all our little fights could ever express.

Always yours,
